Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LearnHive ("LearnHive", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the LearnHive application and website (the "Service"). LearnHive is a social learning platform where students connect, share, and learn together.

By creating an account or using LearnHive, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

2. Who can use LearnHive and children’s privacy

LearnHive is designed for learners aged 8 to 19. Because we serve children, we take extra care with their information. If you are under 18, you should use LearnHive with the knowledge and consent of a parent, guardian, or teacher.

Parents and guardians: you may contact us at any time to review, correct, or delete your child’s information, or to ask us to stop collecting it. Use the contact details in Section 11.

We do not knowingly collect more information from a child than is needed to provide the Service, and we never sell children’s personal information.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account details — your username, email address, password (stored in encrypted form), and age group when you register.
  • Profile information — an optional bio and any details you add to your profile.
  • Content you create — posts, comments, replies, stories, messages, and resources (such as links and titles) that you share on the Service.
  • Activity — the topics and people you follow, and the posts you like.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — basic technical information needed to run the Service, such as log data and the time of your activity.

LearnHive does not collect precise location data, and does not use advertising trackers.

4. The AI Tutor

LearnHive includes an AI Tutor that answers learners’ questions. When you send a message to the AI Tutor, your message is sent to our third-party AI provider, Cohere, which generates the response. Please avoid sharing personal or sensitive information in messages to the AI Tutor. We use this feature only to provide tutoring responses within the app.

5. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and keep you signed in securely.
  • Provide the core features — posting, following, resources, achievements, and the AI Tutor.
  • Show your content and profile to other users as part of the social learning experience.
  • Keep the Service safe, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • Respond to your requests and support questions.

6. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only in these limited ways:

  • With other users — your username, profile, and the content you post are visible to other people on LearnHive. Please only share what you are comfortable being seen.
  • With service providers — companies that help us run the Service, such as our AI provider (Cohere), our hosting and database providers, and similar infrastructure partners, who may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the Service.

7. How we store and protect your information

Your information is stored in a secure database hosted by our infrastructure providers. Passwords are stored using one-way encryption (hashing) and are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your data may be processed or stored on servers located outside South Africa. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your information remains protected in line with POPIA.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and the content associated with it, except where we are required to keep certain records by law.

9. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update your information through the Edit Profile screen.
  • Delete your account and personal information from the Settings screen.
  • Ask us to stop processing your information, by contacting us (see Section 11).

Parents and guardians may exercise these rights on behalf of a child.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top, and where appropriate we will notify you in the app. Continued use of LearnHive after an update means you accept the revised policy.
